# Aegis-Fi

### AI-Powered Autonomous CFO Platform

Aegis-Fi is an AI-driven financial management platform designed to help organizations reduce unnecessary SaaS spending, monitor corporate expenses in real time, and improve financial transparency through intelligent automation.

Our mission is simple:

> **Prevent financial waste before it happens.**

Unlike traditional ERP and expense management systems that analyze spending after transactions occur, Aegis-Fi focuses on proactive financial control by combining Generative AI, SaaS usage analytics, and transparent audit mechanisms.

---

## Problem Statement

As organizations increasingly adopt cloud-based software, SaaS subscriptions have become one of the fastest-growing operational expenses.

However, many companies face challenges such as:

* Unused or underutilized SaaS licenses
* Duplicate subscriptions across departments
* Lack of centralized visibility into software spending
* Delayed expense monitoring and reporting
* Manual financial auditing processes

These inefficiencies lead to significant financial waste and reduce operational efficiency.

---

## Our Solution

Aegis-Fi acts as an **Autonomous CFO Agent** that continuously analyzes organizational spending data and provides actionable financial recommendations.

The platform enables companies to:

* Discover hidden SaaS expenses
* Detect underutilized licenses
* Identify cost-saving opportunities
* Monitor expenses through an integrated dashboard
* Generate AI-powered financial insights
* Maintain transparent audit records

---

## Core Features

### SaaS Subscription Optimization

Analyze SaaS usage patterns and identify:

* Unused licenses
* Low-utilization subscriptions
* Redundant software tools
* Potential cost reductions

The AI agent generates optimization recommendations and estimates expected savings.

---

### AI CFO Agent

A Generative AI-powered financial assistant that:

* Reviews SaaS and expense data
* Produces CFO-level summaries
* Explains financial risks
* Recommends optimization strategies
* Generates executive reports

---

### Real-Time Expense Monitoring

Monitor corporate spending through an integrated dashboard.

Key capabilities include:

* Expense visualization
* Budget tracking
* Spending trend analysis
* Anomaly detection

---

### Audit Transparency Layer

All financial activities are recorded through a tamper-resistant audit mechanism.

Features include:

* Immutable audit records
* Event history tracking
* Financial accountability
* Compliance support
